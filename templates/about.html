About HealthGuard AI

Academic & clinical research overview for Phase 4 Final Project Implementation

Project Mission & Rationale

Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus represents one of the fastest growing global health crises, often progressing asymptomatically over multiple years. Early detection during the prediabetic window can reverse pathology in up to 60% of cases through targeted lifestyle modifications. HealthGuard AI bridges complex biostatistics and front-line triage by providing instantaneous, explainable risk estimates using non-invasive clinical and biometric parameters.

Medical Disclaimer: This web application is an educational and clinical decision-support tool. It does NOT constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Laboratory validation via fasting blood glucose (FBG) or oral glucose tolerance testing (OGTT) by a licensed physician is mandatory.

Cohort Dataset Specifications

Sample Dimensions

  • Total Initial Cohort: 100,000 patient records
  • Clean Deduplicated Cohort: 96,128 records
  • Outcome Prevalence: ~8.8% diabetic (heavily imbalanced)
  • Missing Values: 0 (completely complete records)

Primary Biomarkers

  • Blood Glucose Level (Pearson r = 0.42)
  • Glycated Hemoglobin HbA1c (Pearson r = 0.40)
  • Age (Pearson r = 0.26)
  • Body Mass Index BMI (Pearson r = 0.21)
  • Hypertension & Heart Disease (r = 0.20 / 0.17)

Machine Learning Architecture

• Pre-processing: Continuous variables scaled using StandardScaler; categorical variables encoded with OneHotEncoder (with unseen level fallback).

• Model Ensemble: Tuned Extreme Gradient Boosting (XGBoost) model with scale_pos_weight = 10.33 to account for the ~8.8% minority class prevalence.

• Clinical Threshold Tuning: Decision boundary shifted to 0.58 to yield 88.1% diagnostic recall and an ROC-AUC of 97.6%, guaranteeing that over 9 out of 10 at-risk individuals are caught.

Limitations & Future Enhancements

Current Limitations

  • Cross-sectional observational dataset; lacks longitudinal panel tracking.
  • Genetic markers and family history of diabetes not available in dataset.
  • Smoking history records 36% "No Info" responses.
  • Requires formal prospective clinical trial validation before in-vivo triage.

Planned Roadmap

  • Integration with FHIR / HL7 Electronic Health Record systems.
  • Local tree SHAP explainer graphs dynamically rendered in-browser.
  • Native iOS / Android mobile application with camera scan for lab report OCR.
  • Multi-clinic federated learning pipeline with differential privacy guarantees.
